Two presentations per sprint, in `sprint_NN/`:

- **`planning/`** — Monday, sprint start: the plan for the two weeks until the next Friday (goals, done-when,
  backlog per pair, order of work, targets, risks).
- **`review/`** — Friday, sprint end: **review and retrospective** (achieved / not achieved, demo, numbers against
  target, what went well, what went badly, actions).
