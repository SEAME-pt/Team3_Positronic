## 1. Safety rules (manual)

| # | Rule |
| :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Never stand under any axis of the arm. |
| 2 | Do not stay inside the arm's motion range while a program is running or being debugged. |
| 3 | Keep hands out of the workspace while the arm is powered and moving. |
| 4 | Fix the base (LEGO or M4 screws) before use. |
| 5 | Use only official cables and the supplied power adapter. |
| 6 | Power off and unplug before changing any wiring. |
| 7 | Do not disassemble the arm or remove its screws or casing. |
| 8 | Supervise children at all times. |
| 9 | Do not remove or alter nameplates, labels or markings. |
| 10 | Do not install unofficial firmware. |

After any change to the cell (a new tool, sensor or machine), a new risk assessment must be carried
out.

### Sharp and hot surfaces

- The end-effector and other equipment in the cell can cause scratches and punctures.
- The arm is a precision electronic part; it must not be used in a humid environment for long periods.
- The outer shell is photosensitive resin; high temperatures can deform it and cause failure.

### Before turning the power off

Remove, or firmly support, any object held by the gripper or pump. Losing power drops the object.

---

## 2. Transport

- Transport the robot in its original packaging with the supplied cushioning and supports.
- Environment during transport and long-term storage: −20 °C to +55 °C, ≤ 95 % humidity, no
  condensation.
- Follow the direction arrows on the box.
- Fix every part inside the box so nothing can shift.

---

## 3. Storage

- Store the original packaging dry, within −20 °C to +55 °C and ≤ 95 % humidity.
- Do not stack anything on top of the packaging box.

---

## 4. Prohibited uses (manual)

- Medical or life-critical applications.
- Explosive environments.
- Use without a risk assessment.
- Use with an insufficient safety-function level.
- Use outside the stated performance parameters (payload, reach, temperature) — see
  [02_arm_parameters.md](02_arm_parameters.md).

Dispose of the robot at end of life as industrial electronic waste, per local regulations.

---

## 5. Personnel

Installation, operation and maintenance require trained personnel. Faulty units must be repaired by
myCobot technical support. See [11_maintenance.md](11_maintenance.md).
